How the results were measured

Global chronological split; ratings ≥4 are positive preferences. Models rank the full training-positive catalog, excluding products already reviewed. The blend weight was selected on 1,000 validation users. All models use the same frozen training window.

Warm users and items only; sampled test users, seed 42. These are offline ranking results, not proof of sales improvement or overall superiority.
